About Me

I am an Information Systems student at Carlos Hilado Memorial State University (Talisay Campus). My focus sits right where organizational strategy meets digital design — analyzing workflows, structuring data pipelines, and building digital interfaces that solve real organizational bottlenecks.

Through practical client engagements and academic projects, I have developed a holistic end-to-end perspective: from eliciting stakeholder requirements and drafting comprehensive Entity Relationship Diagrams (ERDs) to crafting accessible Figma prototypes and coding interactive web solutions.

I aim to build dependable, beautifully designed systems that make day-to-day operations simpler, faster, and more transparent for the people who rely on them.

Featured Case Study · Information Systems

NVC Integrated Volunteer and Partners Management System

A unified platform built for Negros Volunteers for Change (NVC Foundation). It replaces fragmented manual logs with centralized portals for volunteer coordination, partner engagement, and administrative analytics.

  • Role-tailored access control across 3 specialized portals (Admin, Volunteer, Partner).
  • End-to-end volunteer onboarding, background verification, and project hour logs.
  • Partner organization collaboration dashboards for joint mission logistics.
  • Integrated child protection safeguards and consent checks on public media uploads.
  • Predictive participation metrics and automated reporting exports.
